1. Idea & Approach: Why True Discoveries Beat Basic AI Summaries Core Insight

Most business AI tools today are just summarizers: you paste a 30-page company speech, and the AI repeats whatever good news the CEO said. But company leaders are paid to sound positive. The real business story is almost always hidden in the gap between what executives say and what their audited accounts and store numbers actually show.

ARGUS is not a chatbot or a summarizer. It operates like an independent investigative auditor through six simple actions: Discover (spot unusual gaps), Connect (link executive words with official balance sheets), Calculate (run exact math with no guesswork), Reason (test whether growth is genuine), Challenge (play devil's advocate to find fair explanations), and Prove (deliver clear findings with clickable public links).

Strict Rules: Zero stock tips or investment recommendations (no "buy", "sell", "hold", or price targets). 100% public information only—nothing paid or behind a password.

4. Track & Generality: Eligible Track C Universe Company-Agnostic Research

The research pipeline uses company-agnostic analysis rules for eligible NIFTY 50 companies in Track C. It can extend to other consumer-sector peers where permitted and where comparable public data is available:

A. Factory Brands (FMCG Makers)

E.g., Hindustan Unilever, Britannia, Dabur, Nestle, Bikaji (outside NIFTY 50).
Checks if higher revenue is just from raising prices while unit volumes are sluggish, or if warehouse stock is piling up as distributors take longer to clear products.

B. Retail Stores & Jewellery (Non-Volume Disclosers)

E.g., Trent (Westside/Zudio), Titan (Jewellery/Watches), V2 Retail, DMart.
Retailers do not report volume in kilograms. ARGUS tracks whether opening new stores creates an illusion of growth while older stores experience maturing sales throughput.

6. Self-Falsification: The "Change-My-Mind" Rule Counterargument & Disproof

ARGUS actively guards against confirmation bias through two built-in algorithmic sanity checks:

1. The Fair Alternative (Devil's Advocate)

The agent actively tests honest operational explanations: "Did inventory rise because management bought raw materials ahead of inflation, or did new stores require baseline stock?"

2. The Exact Disproof Condition (Measurable)

The system sets an exact milestone that disproves its concern: "Disproven if the company delivers two consecutive quarters of verified volume growth >+4.0% with unpaid bills shrinking."
